# Define the first playable Coaching Week

## Question

What is the smallest end-to-end opponent week in which a coach examines Evidence, makes at least three consequential Coaching Decisions, sees those decisions affect Friday, and can review their quality afterward?

## Resolution

The shipped Week 8 slice is that week: Evidence and Scouting Hypotheses (exactly three priorities plus one accepted risk), Game Plan answers, contact-capped practice, Thursday authority events (eligibility, staff, environment, film deadline, emergency process), prepared Friday policies plus Match Day decisions, and Decision Review that grades process apart from the 20–3 outcome. Canonical UI-3 copy remains the visual/behavior authority.
