# Set safety, rules, and data boundaries

## Question

Which availability, academic, medical-process, environmental, and jurisdictional data may the Head Coach see or change, which authorities own each restriction, and which product actions must be impossible?

## Resolution

The Head Coach may see Player Availability (participation, owning authority, scope, checkpoint) and may change the Game Plan around it. Guidance/registrar owns academic eligibility status; the Athletic Trainer (or other healthcare authority) owns Participation Restrictions; the Head Coach cannot certify eligibility or clear a restriction, and the product has no diagnosis field. Emergency and environmental process stay school-owned checklists, not inferred medical or weather clearance. Jurisdiction Rule Sets are versioned by association, season, competition, and level. Live Program Records additionally require School Approval, a separate Data Context, Audit Events, and TSLAC-aligned retention; the current Convex week store is simulation-only (ADR 0003).
