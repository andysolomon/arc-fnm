# Design the coach-validation pilot

## Question

What mix of 8–12 programs, weekly observation protocol, measures, and go/no-go thresholds would demonstrate that the first playable changes real decision process before anyone claims that it helps teams win?

## Resolution

Recruit 8–12 programs contrasting staff size, school size, competitive level, and at least two jurisdictions. Walk one current-state opponent week and one consequential game decision (baseline), then — only after School Approval — a manual Program Workspace week. Primary measures are time-to-decision, Evidence retrieval, plan-to-practice traceability, preventable rules errors, repeated use, and blinded retrospective process quality; win-rate is not a success measure. Thresholds are frozen in `docs/phase-5-pilot-protocol.md`. The protocol has not been executed; the Program Workspace is NO-GO until live-data controls exist.
