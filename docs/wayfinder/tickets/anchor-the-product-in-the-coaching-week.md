# Anchor the product in the Coaching Week

## Question

Which recurring high-school football coaching decisions have enough primary-source support to anchor the product, and what safety or evidence limits must constrain them?

## Resolution

Use a weekly decision loop: review evidence, form a Scouting Hypothesis, allocate limited practice reps, confirm Player Availability, prepare game-day policies, execute, and conduct a Decision Review. The research supports film/scouting, constrained practice planning, roster development and availability, safety process, jurisdiction-specific compliance, and game-state preparation. It does not support automated medical judgment, universal state rules, or direct transfer of NFL numerical thresholds to high school.
