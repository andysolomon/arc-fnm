# Define the simulation role and authority

## Question

When the user says they want to play as a coach or General Manager, which football and program decisions belong to each role, and may one Career combine both roles without making staff delegation meaningless?

## Resolution

General Manager authority is not important to the first playable and is deliberately deferred. The initial Simulation Experience centers the Head Coach; an executive role should return only after a concrete gameplay need establishes its distinct decisions and delegation boundaries.
