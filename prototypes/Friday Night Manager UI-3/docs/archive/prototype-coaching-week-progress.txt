- Nav items and all new controls are semantic <button>s with aria-current and a visible
  focus ring (style-focus, 3px rgba(0,114,245,.35-.45)).
- Global Continue now routes to the next unresolved mandatory decision (stage-driven),
  labelled with its destination, instead of jumping straight to Match Day.
- Wired previously dead Scouting affordances: "Open film room" -> Film Room tab,
  "Reassign" -> Assignments tab.
- Design system gained: Pending Decision card, Evidence/sample chips, Confidence
  indicator, Linked-object trace (with the Unseen/Introduced/Repped/Rehearsed scale),
  Practice period block, Hard constraint (with authority + status vocabulary),
  Accepted risk, Decision Review row. Plus four new rules covering non-color status
  meaning, no dead controls, and traceable derived values.

Phase 1 - Coaching Week hub
- New "Week" nav item, first in the list, and the initial screen (screen:'week').
  Inbox demoted to a supporting surface.
- Week hub contains: opponent/stakes header, Monday-Saturday stage timeline (every chip
  navigates to that day's screen), one dominant Next Decision card with due time and a
  "Why now" footer, preparation objectives with traceable readiness, a constraints rail
  naming the deciding authority, an accepted-risk slot, staff notes, and
  "What changed since yesterday" with jump links.
- Week state model added: stage (evidence|plan|practice|disruption|friday|review),
  selHyp, risk, answers, blocks, planLocked, rtFix. Readiness is derived from blocks,
  never edited directly. Reset week restores the seeded baseline.
- Nav badge on Week shows the count of unresolved hard constraints.

Phase 2 - Evidence-driven Film Room
- Scouting gained four tabs: Overview (preserved verbatim), Film Room, Hypotheses,
  Assignments.
- 32 seeded Central Catholic clips, each with game, situation (down/distance/hash/yard),
  personnel, formation, motion, concept, result, angle availability, a Soto staff note,
  and a hypothesis relationship (supports / contradicts / neutral).
- Film Room: three filter groups (side, situation, tendency incl. "Untagged"), a live
  sample line that reports visible clip count, games represented, and support/against
  within the current filter; clip list with per-clip relationship dot; viewer with
  placeholder frame, Tight/Wide toggle limited to the angles that exist for that clip,
  full metadata grid, staff note, and an editable coach note per clip.
- Coaches can re-tag any clip against any of the four hypotheses. Edits are marked
  "edited" and feed back into the hypothesis support/against counts, so the sample is
  always the user's own.
- Hypotheses: four cards - power tendency (strong), sprint-out (moderate), Cover 3
  leverage (strong), return game (low sample). Each shows the conditional statement,
  snaps, games, live support/against counts, confidence with a plain-language reason,
  what evidence is missing, and a drill-down that filters the Film Room to its clips.
- Gate enforced: exactly three priorities, then the leftover tendency must be explicitly
  accepted as this week's risk. Attempting a fourth priority is refused with an
  explanation rather than silently ignored. Accepting the risk advances the week to the
  Game Plan stage and updates the Week hub and header.
- Assignments: three scout tasks with real staff choices; each choice states its cost
  (who misses which practice period), so reassignment is a tradeoff not a preference.

Phase 4 - Constrained practice allocator
- Training is now two tabs: Practice Plan (default) and Development. The old week cards,
  individual development, unit emphasis, fatigue rail, and intensity control are preserved
  verbatim under Development; nothing was deleted.
- Practice Plan keeps the Monday-Thursday rhythm as four day cards. Each day states its
  pads level (Helmets / Full pads / Shells / Walk-through), total minutes, and a fixed
  script the coach does NOT control (4 periods per day, shown muted). Below the fixed
  script sits the discretionary "Opponent plan" area.
- Eight ten-minute priority blocks, distributed MON 2 / TUE 3 / WED 2 / THU 1 - so the
  slot count is the scarcity, and Tuesday's three live slots are the real bottleneck.
- Placement is select-then-place: "Place a block" on an objective arms it, a banner names
  what is being placed, and every open slot becomes a target. Clicking an open slot with
  nothing armed explains what to do rather than doing nothing.
- Each placed block shows unit, expected reps, contact tone, and the responsible coach,
  plus an inline note when the rep is degraded. Tuesday blocks can be toggled Live/Thud.
- Rep + readiness math (all traceable, never a single opaque score):
  reps = 8 live / 6 padless / 4 Thursday walk-through.
  weight = contact objective: 1.0 live, 0.6 thud, 0.4 Mon-Wed air, 0.3 Thursday;
           non-contact objective: 1.0 Mon-Wed, 0.7 Thursday.
  Unseen 0 / Introduced <1 / Repped <2 / Rehearsed >=2, and a contact objective with no
  live Tuesday rep is hard-capped at Repped with that sentence printed on the row.
- Constraint consequences are real, not cosmetic: o1 live blocks lose 2 reps because McCoy
  cannot take contact and Dunn runs the scout counter; o5 blocks are worth half credit
  while Kowalski's replacement is unresolved (Phase 5 clears this via rtFix).
- Every objective row shows: source answer + owning coach, readiness, reps against the
  answer's target, one line per placed block explaining what it produced, and the affected
  Student-Athletes with Active / No contact / Ineligible status (dot + word, never color
  alone).
- Right rail: plan actions (Undo history, Staff plan, Save draft, Lock), the three
  authority-owned constraints, and "Where the reps went" unit balance with a warning when
  a unit gets nothing.
- Gate: with fewer than three answers set, the allocator explains that blocks are spent on
  answers and routes to the Game Plan; open slots and the Staff plan / Lock buttons are
  disabled with the reason stated.
- Lock requires all eight blocks placed. Locking freezes the grid, advances the week to the
  disruption stage, and prints "What Friday looks like" - Prepared / Thin / Knowingly
  uncovered, each with its rep evidence.
- Week hub now reads the same derived readiness (no more block-count proxy), and the
  Training nav item carries a badge with the number of unplaced blocks during the practice
  stage.

POST-PHASE-5 UX PASS — guided flow and direct manipulation
Implemented: 2026-08-02 (afternoon)
================================================================

Driven by first-use feedback: the app must drive the coach to the next
blocker, and lists must not show the same thing in two places.

1. Smart Continue button (header)
   nextStep computed from real state, not the stage marker. Chain:
     Prioritize concerns n/3 -> Name the accepted risk -> Set answers n/3
     -> Fix the conflicted answer -> Place blocks n/8 / Lock the practice plan
     -> BLOCKER No legal right tackle -> BLOCKER Decide the RT package
     -> Confirm Friday personnel -> Match Day
   Button names the action, navigates to exact screen+tab+phase, tooltip says
   why. Blocker states turn the button red. Tactics nav badge turns red with
   the unresolved count while the Depth Chart blocks Friday.

2. Inline risk decision
   The Hypotheses gate banner lists the remaining candidate(s) with an
   "Accept as this week's risk" button, so Continue's landing spot contains
   the action (it used to sit below the fold).

3. Game Plan stack behavior
   Answered concern cards sink to the bottom; next unanswered surfaces on top.
   Completion banner when all 3 are set: green (clean) with "Open the practice
   plan", red variant when an answer conflicts with the active front.

4. Objectives list dedupe (Training)
   Objectives with >=1 block leave "Objectives competing for the plan" - they
   exist only on the day grid. Header shows "n left / m on the script";
   empty state when everything is allocated. Place button disabled at target
   ("Target met"). Rows sorted: unallocated first, risk/off-board last.

5. Depth chart drag-and-drop (real)
   Bench rows are native drag sources (dataTransfer text/plain = bench index);
   every slot is a drop target; swap puts the displaced starter on the bench
   (Kowalski keeps an ineligible flag). Slot cards sized 9.4% of field width
   (min-width 520px on the field) so drop targets never overlap. Out-of-
   position starters get an amber "OG PLAYING RT" flag. Dropping a legal body
   on RT resolves Step 1 of the Thursday panel; panel option buttons perform
   the same swaps (Webb / Ruiz / slide-the-line).

6. Practice plan kanban
   Placed blocks drag between days (payload 'block:<id>'); objective rows drag
   from the list onto a day ('obj:<oid>'). Day columns highlight during drag,
   refuse drops at capacity; moves recompute live/tempo/reps for the new day
   and go through the undo stack.

Known-good end-to-end (self-tested in preview): prioritize 3 -> accept risk
inline -> 3 answers (cards sink, banner appears) -> Continue to Training ->
drag objective to MON -> drag block MON->TUE (goes Live) -> counters track.

PHASE 6 — Preparation-aware Match Day
Implemented: 2026-08-02
================================================================

State (all in WEEK_SEED, so Reset Week clears the game)

Match Day now has three faces
  Locked out (stage before friday): explains why kickoff is gated, Continue
    button routes to the real next step. The old auto-running Q3 script is gone.
  Decision Room (stage=friday, pregame): the four Situational Policies get a
    last confirm (same polList state as Tactics), beside a "What you take onto
    the field" readiness recap (Prepared/Thin/Uncovered from the real derived
    objState), the accepted-risk strip, and Take the field. Taking the field
    snapshots {objective levels, answers, rtFix, RT name, risk, policies} as
    the game context.
  Live/final: preserved scoreboard/field/feed/Quick Adjust/speed controls, plus
    quarter label is real (Q1-Q4/OT), feed rows carry trace tags, Skip is now
    "Next call" (runs to the next key situation), FINAL state adds a Decision
    review button and sets stage='review' (Phase 7 hangs off this).

Engine (buildGame in the logic class)
  Deterministic, no RNG: variance is scripted. ~12 key situations across four
  quarters; 6 are coach decisions in a Key Situation panel over the field (game
  state chips + live score, evidence-from-the-week line, staff voice, 2-4
  choices each with its stated cost). The rest are annotated auto-plays.
  Situations: opening KO return (h4/o4), the power test (h1/o1, decision),
  RT protection 3rd&7 (branches on rtFix: promote/simplify/switch/accept),
  4th&2 policy call, sprint-out test (h2/o2), two-minute clock/timeout call
  (honors the coordinator-autonomy policy — Tillman pre-checks the front if it
  is his), halftime, the trips-flood shot (h3/o3, framing changes if the answer
  was switched or never installed), the Q3 power drive, Q4 TD + conversion
  policy call, and a score-aware closer (defense protects a lead / offense
  4th&6, framing computed from the live score; OT paths exist so no tie ends
  the game).
  Readiness gates outcomes without guaranteeing them: Rehearsed sprint-out
  contain still gets beaten once by a missed tackle (execution, tagged as
  such), and an Unseen power front still gets one Okafor solo TFL ("execution
  beat scheme"). Accepted risk is tagged amber wherever it cashes (Malone
  returns, uncovered flood window, unprotected five-step).
  Quick Adjust reaches branches at resolve time: Blitz Heavy changes the Q3
  power stop and the closing pressure call, Pound the Rock changes the ground
  option in the flood situation, Air It Out enables the closing boot.

Both validation scenarios produce different games: Scenario A (defense-first)
wins on stops but bleeds return-game field goals; Scenario B (offense/ST)
hits the flood but eats power touchdowns. Either can win or lose at the
closing decision.

Richer decision log (choose())
  Every chosen Key Situation now logs: dec id, option index, chips, the
  evidence string AS SHOWN AT THE TIME, staff line + speaker, chosen option,
  score at the moment of the call, the resulting play sequence (text + trace
  tags), and the points swing across that sequence. advance() keeps logging
  tagged plays (incl. every "Accepted risk" cash) as before.

New screen: screen='review' (no nav item — entered via the final Match Day
button, the Schedule result card, the Week hub review card, Continue, and the
SAT timeline chip; STAGES review row now routes here instead of schedule)
  Header: final score, WIN/LOSS chip, and the framing line (scoreboard is one
    output of many). Empty state if no game is on film yet.
  Decision timeline: one card per logged decision — when/chips, WHAT YOU KNEW
    THEN (the pregame evidence verbatim), the staff voice at the time, the
    choice, the actual play-by-play with trace tags, then three separate rows:
    Decision (staff process read: Sound / Debatable / Poor process + rationale
    computed from policy/readiness/evidence at decision time, never from the
    result), Execution (first execution tag or "ran as called"), Result
    (points swing on the sequence). The coach adds their own read via three
    pills; agreement/split with the staff read is stated in words.
  Process rubric (procOf in renderVals) covers all 8 situation ids and
    branches on the snapshot ctx — e.g. trusting Rehearsed fits is Sound while
    trusting Introduced fits is Debatable; the hard count is Poor process with
    a certain five-yard downside; prevent at +3 or less is Poor process while
    prevent up 4+ is Sound. Good calls can lose and bad calls can win, and the
    labels don't move.
  Accepted-risk retrospective: the hypothesis statement, every logged cash
    with timestamp, and a verdict line (never cashed = luck, not proof it was
    free). Framed as reviewing Monday's decision, not Friday's luck.
  Lessons: 4-6 candidates computed from the actual game — risk cash count,
    first sound-call-bad-sequence, first non-sound-call-good-sequence, an
    rtFix-specific lesson, a Rehearsed-still-failed execution lesson, a
    delegated-policy lesson. Save up to 3; a 4th is refused with an
    explanation. Saved lessons appear on the completed Week hub (What changed
    feed) and in the next-opponent card.
  Westfield Courier: state-aware headline + 2-sentence story (score, the real
    RT program decision, next opponent). Footer states the paper grades
    results while the room grades decisions — it is never the evaluator.
  Close out the week: disabled until >=1 lesson saved (reason stated), then
    returns to a completed Week hub — timeline all green, header "Saturday ·
    Week complete", Next Decision card becomes the Week 9 Riverside preview
    with a Reopen-the-review secondary action.

Schedule is now state-aware after the final: hero card shows the final score,
tiebreak note, and a Decision Review button; the Week 8 row gets a W/L result;
Week 9 becomes "next"; record line and district standings recompute for either
outcome (win = Westfield #1 at 5-0; loss = Central 8-0 and Westfield #2).

Known gaps carried into Phase 8
  Lessons are seeded candidates only — no free-text lesson entry.
  The review's user ratings (revRate) are not surfaced anywhere outside the

  Old this.SCRIPT array is still dead data — remove in the 8.4 cleanup.
